What is Project Hestia?

Hestia is a community OG Fortnite emulator project built around revisiting the original Fortnite experience, with a winter-inspired home for the project and its players.

How do I start playing?

Get the Hestia launcher for Windows, then follow its setup instructions. The community is the place to check for supported game builds, play sessions, and help getting started.

Can I use it on Mac or console?

The launcher shown here is for Windows. Mac and console downloads are not provided on this site.

Where can I find updates and support?

Join the Hestia community for project announcements, setup help, and other players.

Is Hestia affiliated with Epic Games?

No. Hestia is an independent community project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Games. Fortnite and its associated marks belong to Epic Games.
